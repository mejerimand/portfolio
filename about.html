About

Hi! My name is Maja Riemann and welcome to my website.

I am a designer working at the intersection of technology, society, and ecology, with a passion for expanding collective imagination and opening up space for multiple alternative futures. My practice is informed by theory but grounded in making and doing: I love to give form to sensuous and meaningful interactions between humans, technologies, and natural ecosystems. I draw on speculative and participatory approaches to help others navigate complexities in playful ways, nurture our imaginations, and bring forth ideas that support new ways of thinking and being.
