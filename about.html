About

I am a designer working at the intersection of technology, society, and ecology, with a passion for expanding collective imagination and opening up space for multiple alternative futures. My practice is informed by theory and grounded in making: I love to give form to sensuous and meaningful interactions between humans, technologies, and natural ecosystems. I draw on speculative and participatory approaches to help navigate complexities in playful ways, nurture our imaginations, and bring forth ideas that support new ways of thinking and being.
