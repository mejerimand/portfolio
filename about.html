About

I am a design researcher for whom design is about imagining and creating possibilities beyond what we currently know and see. My practice is grounded in research-through-design and informed by feminist, decolonial, and more-than-human perspectives, seeking ways to honour multiple ways of being and knowing beyond conventional paradigms. Through designing thoughtful, material, and embodied encounters, interventions, and provocations, I explore the complex relationships between humans, technology, and our broader environment, opening up spaces of possibility toward meaningful change for our collective futures.

I am currently looking for work as a Ph.D. in the field of HCI and design research, working with our common imagination, focusing on the epistemological and ontological implications of AI in everyday life.
