Arcane Designing Cards is a deck of prompting cards that supports playful imagination. They are designed to destabilise participants' ontological expectations of possibilities and foster ideas for radical alternatives. It consists of three categories: a fantastical ontological world condition, a mundane everyday practice, and a magical possibility. Together, these cards establish a structured yet open-ended system of worldbuilding. By using fantastical world conditions and magical possibilities, they help people's imagination depart from common assumptions about the future and move beyond restrictions put on the imagination.
