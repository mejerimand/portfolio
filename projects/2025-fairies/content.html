The Feral Cyborg Fairies is a material fabulation that speculates on radically alternative AI. It draws on fairy folklore to explore how we can imagine technology that embraces the hybrid messiness of our realities, refusing the choice between techno-deterministic narratives and nostalgic fantasies of a pure 'natural' and 'original' way of living.

One of the challenges in imagining alternative futures around technology, especially technologies as debated as AI, is how to support people in moving beyond dominant narratives. These, often reductive but strong, narratives prevent us from examining the underlying assumptions on which we design technologies. In response, this project engaged with folkloric practices as a design strategy for questioning current logics and proposing alternative relations between humans, technology, and ecological ecosystems.
