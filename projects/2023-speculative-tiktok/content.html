This project speculated on the future of mobility in a future that extrapolated on the current expanding age-gap overlapping with the current trends of automation, robots, and AI.

This speculation resulted in a story about a kidnapping by personal well-being robots, and as a reaction, we also speculated on how to 'hack' such technologies in a world with developed control systems that 'know better' than the user. To further enhance the immersive storytelling, we added subtitles into present-day English from the semi-gibberish slang we used in the video.

The following text is the introduction to the scenario/video: In a future where ... the aging population vastly outnumbers the younger generation, AI caretakers become the primary support, ensuring their well-being and companionship. This symbiotic relationship between elders and machines reshapes society's understanding of care and connection.
