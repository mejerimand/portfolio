This project speculates on the future of mobility in a scenario that extrapolates the current trends of an expanding elderly population and increasing automation through robots and AI. The scenario mixes humour with a dystopian tone to foster constructive debate around mobility, technology, and the shifting demographics.

The following text is the introduction to the scenario: In a future where the aging population vastly outnumbers the younger generation, AI caretakers become the primary support, ensuring their well-being and companionship. This symbiotic relationship between elders and machines reshapes society's understanding of care and connection.
