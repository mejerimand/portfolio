This exhibition presents four scenarios, each exploring a possible future of welfare in Denmark: 1) The safety net spread too thin, 2) Free city states, 3) Platform of community, and 4) Digital infrastructure. I was part of designing the exhibition at the DDC. It was designed for KL and exhibited at their annual summit. Through these diverse scenarios, actors around welfare in Denmark could engage in constructive dialogue about current welfare challenges and the future we want to build.
