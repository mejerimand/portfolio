What if, instead of booking a holiday, you had to apply for one?

It's 2044, and the tourism industry has fundamentally changed. Instead of travellers choosing where to go, the local ecosystems of the destinations choose who gets to come.

This speculative project imagines a booking system run by "Earth AI", a stewarding intelligence connected to a destination's ecosystem, built environment, and local population. It decides which tourists are welcome based on their intentions rather than their wallet. It's a provocation on what tourism could look like if it moved from extraction to reciprocity, from taking to caring, and from human agency to more-than-human agency.
