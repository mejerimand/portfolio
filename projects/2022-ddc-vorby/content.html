Vorby was an experiential scenario exploring a preferred future for young people's well-being in Denmark. Using sound, touch, taste, stories, artefacts, videos and physical places, Vorby made an alternative future concrete and relatable. It materialised new norms around nature, trust, care, and belonging that people could experience and explore with their own bodies. This enabled new kinds of conversations around the complex challenges of the mental health crisis, and mobilised people to act together in new ways and across organisations and silos.

Through my role as a project assistant at the DDC, I contributed to designing the scenario. We designed it through a co-creation process with over 150 partners and actors from across the mental health landscape. It culminated in a well-being conference where we invited decision-makers and innovators to time travel with us to Vorby. Later, the scenario lived on as a digital resource that could be used by actors (old and new) in working with well-being.
