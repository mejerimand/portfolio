Following my bachelor's, I joined the Danish Design Center (DDC) as a project assistant, working on multiple projects and with different stakeholders in creating visions of the future.

My role as a project assistant included taking on many different hats, such as writing and creating narratives of the futures, scenarios, and people that lived in these. I was also responsible for creating functioning speculative artefacts to increase the experienced immersion into these scenarios, bringing my design background into practice. Below, I highlight some of my work.

Project: Future of Welfare, 2050

This project is four scenarios exploring the future of welfare in Denmark: The safety net spread too thin, Free city states, Platform of community, Digital infrastructure. The images below display the exhibition of the scenarios collected.

Beyond working on the narratives, texts, and setup of this exhibition, I employed my skills as a designer in creating my first artefact for DDC, the HelpHand 4, which speculated on a future where 'warm hands' from care-workers were replaced by synthetic (robotic) hands.
