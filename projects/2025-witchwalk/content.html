The Witch Walks are guided, experiential workshops in movement. They combine walking with storytelling, sensory attunement, gathering, hands-on making, and playful sense-making. Each Witch Walk culminates in the creation of one or more amulets. It is designed as a more-than-human sensitising activity to explore magic's potential to contribute to the field of More-than-Human Design. It incorporates four design considerations synthesised from a literature review on building more-than-human sensibilities: 1) engaging the body and senses, 2) using artefacts to attune rather than control, 3) balancing imagination with meaningful encounter with more-than-human agencies, and 4) prolonged engagement.
