My bachelor's thesis explored how design can support people's relationship to their own sexuality. It was carried out in collaboration with Copenhagen Pride and Peech.

Using the Social Implication Design method, the project began by mapping the future context around sexuality and identity in Denmark through various participatory design methods, identifying self-empowerment and supporting people in improving their relationship to their own sexuality as a valuable point of intervention. From this, we formed a concrete design proposal aimed at fostering curiosity, safety, and acceptance, which became the basis for designing possible interventions.

Eight concepts were developed, from which two were further prototyped and validated: Queergo, a block-based social game, and a Christmas calendar that gently challenges one's own assumptions, as well as providing fun tips and prizes. This project did not conclude in a single concept, but the calendar was further developed beyond the project in collaboration with Peech and the Copenhagen Pride Youth Group.
